August 15, 2026

IGB Team at Indicon 2026!

We brought our games to Moscow’s festival of indie games and fandoms.

Read full storyShow less

Hello hello everyone! It's our last day in Moscow, so we wanted to share some summary of Indicon!

Over 20 people went through our table, each of them thoughtfully and intelligently read the text and sometimes even double-checked other outcomes of the dice roll.

There were cosplayers, market sellers, event volunteers, and regular visitors. And from what we remember, they all left satisfied. There were some minor hiccups, but almost no one left without getting a good outcome on the first roll.

It's still very gratifying, because getting someone to sit down and read calmly when there's chaos and hustle around you is incredibly precious.

We didn't make much money on merch; after all, we didn't have any new items, and people were coming for the game, not expecting to see merch. But we still earned about 80$.

Many conclusions were made about what needed to be added and fixed in the demo. But I hope there won't be any delays, and the demo Luck Cursed will be released this year, and you'll support the full release financially.

Thank you for visiting Indicon!

About the studio

Insult GoatBro Team

vIgnitio founded our team on April 30, 2017. Our first project was an Undertale fan game called Facing Demons. Since then, we’ve explored new genres and formats, from puzzle games to visual novels.

We’ve been travelling to events together for three years. We placed third at the 2024 Underparty Gamejam in Moscow, 17th at Kapella Jam 3 in 2026, and 22nd at Anivisual Jam 4 in 2022.
